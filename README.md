# Endometriosis-dataset
Endometriosis Clinical and Demographic Dataset

Feature	Description
Age	Age of the individual at the time of data collection (in years).
BMI	Body Mass Index — a measure of body fat based on height and weight (kg/m²).
Cycle_Length	Average length of the individual’s menstrual cycle (in days).
Age_of_Menarche	Age at which the individual had their first menstrual period.
Dysmenorrhea_Score	Severity score for menstrual pain (dysmenorrhea), typically rated on a scale (e.g., 0–10).
Pelvic_Pain_Score	Severity of chronic pelvic pain, rated by the individual (e.g., 0–10).
Dyspareunia_Score	Severity score of pain experienced during sexual intercourse.
Dyschezia_Score	Score representing the severity of pain during bowel movements.
Urinary_Symptoms_Score	Composite score representing urinary symptoms (e.g., urgency, frequency, pain).
Family_History	Binary indicator or categorical variable showing presence of endometriosis in family (e.g., Yes/No or 0/1).
Infertility_Status	Indicates whether the individual has experienced infertility (typically Yes/No or 0/1).
CA_125_Level	Blood level of cancer antigen 125 — a marker often elevated in endometriosis and other gynecologic conditions.
CRP_Level	C-reactive protein level — a general marker of inflammation in the body.
Mental_Health_Score	Assessment of mental health status or distress, usually from a standardized questionnaire or scale.
Endometriosis_Stage - Presence or Absence of Endometriosis  0 = No Endometriosis, 1=eEndometriosis
